# Attribution Policy

Semantic Cache is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). That license does not remove
the obligations attached to third-party material included in the repository.

Before adding copied or adapted code, documentation, data, model artifacts, images, or other
material:

1. Confirm that its license permits the intended use and is compatible with this repository.
2. Preserve required copyright, license, and notice text.
3. Record the source URL, exact version or commit, license, local destination, and whether the
   material was copied or modified.
4. Put substantial third-party notices in a dedicated notice file beside the material, or in a
   root `NOTICE` file when the license requires it.
5. Explain non-obvious attribution in the pull request.

Do not add material whose source or license cannot be established. Dependency manifests and
lockfiles identify packages but do not replace any attribution or notice required by a package's
license.

No third-party source material is currently vendored or adapted in this repository.
