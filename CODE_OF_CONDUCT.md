# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Project participants must communicate respectfully, welcome differing levels of experience,
give actionable feedback, and focus discussion on the work. Examples of unacceptable behavior
include harassment, threats, discrimination, sustained disruption, sexualized content, and
publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone
is representing the project. It applies equally to contributors and maintainers.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a routine concern visible in a public thread, use GitHub's reporting controls and avoid
repeating harmful content. For a sensitive report, use the private reporting route described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and prefix the report title with `[Conduct]`. Do not include more
personal information than the maintainers need to evaluate the report.

Maintainers will review the available context, hear affected parties where practical, and choose
a proportionate response. Responses may include a clarification, warning, content removal,
temporary participation restriction, or permanent ban. Maintainers must recuse themselves when
they are directly involved in the reported incident.

Security and conduct reports share a private transport only; conduct reports are evaluated under
this policy rather than as software vulnerabilities.
